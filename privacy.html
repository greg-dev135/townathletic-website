Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Town Athletic Club ("Town", "we", "us") is a training club under construction in Atlanta, Georgia. This policy explains what information we collect through townathletic.com and our priority list, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the priority list, we collect the information you choose to give us:

  • Your first and last name and email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide one
  • Your answers about founding membership interest, your training, and equipment you'd like to see
  • Your marketing choices (email and text), and the date and time you signed up

We don't use advertising trackers on this site. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

How we use it

  • To send priority list updates: construction progress, Founder Pre-Sale events, membership registration and our opening date
  • To invite you to give input on equipment and join open forums about the club
  • To understand what our future members want, including the equipment requests you share
  • To send occasional news and offers by email, only if you opted in
  • To send text messages, only if you opted in and gave a phone number

Text messages

Text updates are optional and never required to join the list. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the list, such as our database and email providers, and only so they can provide that service to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're on the priority list and for as long as it's useful for our opening and membership communications. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email
  • Reply STOP to opt out of texts
  • Email hello@townathletic.com to see, correct or delete your information

Security

Signups are stored in a secured database that the public website can add to but cannot read. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to the people who run Town.

Children

The priority list is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be shared with the priority list.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@townathletic.com.
